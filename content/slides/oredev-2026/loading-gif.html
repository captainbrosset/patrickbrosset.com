--- title: Loading GIFs notes: | Behold the loading GIF! Ajax became a very popular technique in the early 2000s. And I was definitely here for it. But until then, the browser had been responsible for navigating between pages and it also gave users a clear indication that something was happening when it navigated. Status bar updates, loading indicator somewhere in the title bar. Once we started loading content asynchronously, that indication disappeared. And we needed a way to tell the user: hang on, something is happening here. So we turned to animated GIFs. And we went a bit crazy with them. We'd load relatively heavy images, sometimes 100 KB or more, just to display a little spinning animation while something else loaded. Again, we weren't being stupid. We had created a new interaction pattern, and that had created a new UX problem: how do you tell the user that something is happening? So we improvised with the tools of the time. ---
