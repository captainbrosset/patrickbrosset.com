Lyra Rebane used modern CSS features to recreate a fully functioning x86 CPU 100% in CSS. Jane Ori, amazing CSS experimenter, pushes CSS specs to their limits, often exposing things that should never have been possible in the first place, like revealing your IP address in CSS only. They push against the boundaries of new features. They take something that's only just becoming possible and abuse it until it does things nobody originally expected. They don't take "no" for an answer. They ask, "What if?" ---
