--- title: Sliced images and tables notes: | I didn't have any programming knowledge, so my path into it was very visual. I'd create designs in Photoshop, slice them into tiny pieces, and then painstakingly put those pieces back together in HTML using tables, spacer GIFs, and hard-coded positions and sizes. The goal was to reproduce the original image exactly. Not responsiveness. Not adapting to user preferences. Pixel-perfect everywhere. ---
