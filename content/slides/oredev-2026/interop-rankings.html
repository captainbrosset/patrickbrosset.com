--- title: Interop rankings notes: | Talking about Interop, the Firefox team has created a tool which lets developers rank their favorite interoperability proposals. After the proposal window has closed, you can use the tool to rank the proposal in the order which you prefer, to reflect the things which impact you the most today. And we use the results to help inform the Interop selection process. This year, many developers even published their rankings publicly, which was wonderful to see. Blog posts like these help spread awareness, encouraging more developers to participate, and also help us get even more nuance in the feedback we receive. ---
