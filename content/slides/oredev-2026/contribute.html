--- layout: slide.njk title: Fun never gets old - Oredev 2026 - Contribute signals notes: | Finally, contribute signals. Two ways I want to highlight: 1. Developer-signals. It's a repo that collects thumbsups and use cases from developers, about features that aren't yet available on all browsers. It helps us understand what matters to developers. You can engage with this through places like MDN and Can I Use, so very simple. 2. Interop. There's also the Interop project. Every year, developers have an opportunity to tell browser vendors about the interoperability problems that hurt them most. This happens in September/October, so too late for this year, but there's always next year. Browser vendors and other organizations then collaborate on improving those areas. ---
