--- title: The legacy of title notes: | The web has had a primitive that a11y specialists, standards people, and devs themselves have pushed back against for decades: the title attribute. You can use it as a tooltip mechanism, but it has significant limitations. The resulting experience can be problematic for keyboard and touch users, and developers have no control over what the browser-generated tooltip looks like, when it appears, how big it is, etc. And yet the need hasn't gone away. Developers still need ways to provide supplementary information, particularly for things like icon buttons. ---
