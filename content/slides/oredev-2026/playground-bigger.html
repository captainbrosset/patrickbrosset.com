--- title: The playground got bigger notes: | So, the playground in which we experiment has gotten bigger, not smaller. But it's still a playground. So, if anything, we should play more, not less. An application platform which stops evolving to go where the users are is a platform that's heading toward obsolescence. It slows down. It becomes complacent. It stops addressing unmet needs. We've kind of seen what happens when that virtuous cycle stops. After IE6, Microsoft dramatically slowed investment in the browser. The assumption was essentially that the browser problem had been solved. It hadn't. The web was only getting started. ---
